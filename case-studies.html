Case Study 02

Scaling Distributor Acquisition for a PCD Pharma Company

Building a predictable digital pipeline for franchise and distributor enquiries.

Business Type

PCD Pharma Company (Franchise Model)

The Challenge

Inconsistent distributor enquiries and heavy reliance on word-of-mouth for expansion.

Timeline

90-day activation and scale-up

Step 01

Challenge

The company had a strong product catalogue and a growing franchise network, but lacked a structured digital system to attract new PCD partners consistently.

No digital funnel for franchise enquiries
Enquiries scattered across calls and WhatsApp
No qualification criteria for new partners
Weak digital brand presence
Step 02

Solution

PharmaScale built a distributor acquisition engine combining:

PCD-focused landing pages
Google & Meta ad campaigns
WhatsApp lead capture & qualification
CRM for enquiry management
Digital product catalogue
Analytics dashboard for ROI
Step 03

Implementation

1

Funnel Design & Campaign Setup

Built conversion-optimized landing pages and launched multi-channel ad campaigns targeting prospective PCD partners.

2

WhatsApp Qualification Workflow

Automated capture of key business details (location, investment capacity, existing distribution) before routing to sales.

3

CRM Integration & Team Training

Centralized all enquiries in a CRM, set up lead assignment rules, and trained the sales team on the new workflow.

4

Optimization & Scale

Refined targeting based on lead quality, expanded to new regions, and scaled ad spend on best-performing channels.

Step 04

Transformation

Before

Word-of-mouth driven
Unpredictable enquiries
No qualification process

After

Predictable digital funnel
Qualified leads only
Data-driven expansion
Step 05

Business Impact

Consistent lead flow
Higher quality enquiries
Faster response time
Geographic expansion
Transparent ROI tracking
Improved sales productivity

Automating Consumer Engagement for a Nutraceutical Brand

Building a connected customer journey from first touch to repeat purchase.

Business Type

D2C Nutraceutical Brand

The Challenge

High one-time purchase rate but poor retention and repeat order frequency.

Timeline

60-day deployment and ongoing optimization

Step 01

Challenge

The brand was acquiring customers effectively through ads but struggled to retain them or drive repeat purchases.

No post-purchase engagement
Manual WhatsApp support
No customer segmentation
Low repeat order rate
Step 02

Solution

PharmaScale implemented a customer engagement system powered by AI and automation:

WhatsApp automation journeys
AI customer assistant
Customer segmentation
Automated email flows
Repeat purchase reminders
Retention analytics
Step 03

Implementation

1

Customer Journey Mapping

Mapped every touchpoint from first ad click to repeat purchase to identify engagement gaps.

2

Automation & AI Setup

Built WhatsApp journeys, set up AI customer assistant, and configured automated email sequences.

3

Segmentation & Personalization

Segmented customers by purchase behavior and personalized offers to increase repeat orders.

4

Continuous Optimization

Monitored retention metrics and refined automation flows based on customer behavior data.

Transformation

Manual support
No retention system

After

AI-driven support
